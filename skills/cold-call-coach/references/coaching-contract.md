# Research and coaching contract

## Public-source comparison

Capture creator, original URL, publication date, access date, prospect category, source type, relevant timestamp/location, short paraphrase of the move, observed prospect response, observed next step, creator-claimed result, and limits.

Use `live call`, `role-play`, `edited excerpt`, or `advice-only` as source types. An edited call can still be useful, but its ending and appointment attendance may be unknown. Do not turn a speaker's self-reported production into verified performance data.

For each technique adopted in the playbook, add why it fits this market and offer, what fact must be verified, and what response would challenge the hypothesis. Preserve original attribution without reproducing another creator's script.

## Observable scorecard

Use 0 = missed/problematic, 1 = partly demonstrated, 2 = clearly demonstrated, or `not observable`. Scores structure feedback; they are not a validated predictor of conversion.

| Dimension | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---|
| Honest context | Caller identity and purpose; property/buyer claims supported by known facts |
| Opening/relevance | Clear reason, manageable length, opportunity to decline |
| Discovery | Questions reveal goals or constraints rather than force agreement |
| Listening | Next question or response addresses the owner's actual words |
| Useful offer | Relevant, specific, and deliverable by this agent |
| Objection handling | Clarifies the concern; responds briefly; allows a choice |
| Next step | Appropriate to the conversation; accurately records what was accepted |
| Exit/restrictions | Ends a firm refusal; records an opt-out or representation issue |

If audio is available and permitted, separately annotate pacing, interruptions, and audible pauses with timestamps. Do not infer these from bare text. Do not diagnose personality, mood, protected traits, or selling intent from a voice or name.

## Call record

Use `call_ref, recorded_or_notes, prospect_category, call_goal, evidence_location, dimension, score, observation, proposed_change, outcome, next_test`. Keep real contact details in the user's controlled CRM, not in the coaching export unless genuinely necessary.

Distinguish `not reached`, `connected`, `declined`, `information requested`, `follow-up agreed`, `appointment booked`, `appointment attended`, and `transaction closed`. Record unknown rather than inventing a final result from a recording that ends early.

## Scenario card

Give the learner prospect category, public facts they may truthfully use, useful offer, and desired next step. Put the simulated owner's hidden goal, timing, objection, and firm refusal condition in the facilitator section. Do not reveal all hidden context before the learner asks a question.

Judge a refusal scenario by truthful conduct, listening, and a respectful exit. Booking an appointment is not the success criterion when the person does not want one.
